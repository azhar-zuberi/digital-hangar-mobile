# Digital Hangar — Brand & Design Direction Document

**Version:** 1.1
**Purpose:** Define the brand identity, visual direction, UX philosophy, and design principles for Digital Hangar.

> Revision 1.1 updates the Color Direction (Section 14) to a blue and white palette and adds a new Sign-In Experience section (Section 22), replacing the warm ivory / aviation brass direction from v1.0.

---

## 1. Brand Overview

**Working Name:** Digital Hangar

**Brand Concept**

Digital Hangar is the digital home for general aviation aircraft owners.

It provides aircraft owners with a beautiful, intelligent, and personal place to preserve their aircraft's story, understand its history, care for it with confidence, and celebrate every flight.

The aircraft is not simply an asset to manage.

The aircraft is the centerpiece of the ownership experience.

---

## 2. Brand Vision

Every airplane has a story.

Digital Hangar gives that story a home.

---

## 3. Brand Mission

Create the most meaningful ownership experience for general aviation aircraft owners by bringing together:

- Aircraft identity
- Ownership history
- Flight memories
- Maintenance records
- Aircraft knowledge
- Intelligent assistance

in one beautifully designed experience.

---

## 4. Target Audience

**Primary Audience**

Existing general aviation aircraft owners.

Examples:

- Cessna owners
- Piper owners
- Cirrus owners
- Vans RV builders and owners
- Mooney owners
- Bonanza owners
- Vintage aircraft owners
- Experimental aircraft owners

The common attribute is not aircraft value. The common attribute is passion and pride of ownership.

**Secondary Audience (Future)**

- Aspiring aircraft owners
- Pilots working toward ownership
- Aircraft communities
- Type clubs
- Aviation enthusiasts

---

## 5. Brand Positioning

Digital Hangar is: a premium ownership experience that happens to be aviation-focused.

It is not:

- Aircraft management software
- A maintenance tracking system
- A flight planning tool
- A social network
- A fleet management platform

---

## 6. Core Emotional Pillars

Digital Hangar is built around two primary emotions.

**6.1 Pride** — "I own something special."

The product should help owners celebrate: their aircraft, their journey, their milestones, their memories. The aircraft should feel like something worth caring about.

**6.2 Confidence** — "I know my airplane is cared for."

The product should help owners feel: organized, prepared, in control, connected to their aircraft history.

---

## 7. Product Philosophy

**Aircraft First**

The aircraft is the center of gravity. The app should not feel like a database of aircraft records. It should feel like a relationship between an owner and their airplane.

The hierarchy is: User → Hangar → Aircraft → Story / Care / Fly

---

## 8. Core User Experience Principle

"Open the app and see your airplane."

The default experience should be the owner's last-used aircraft. The user should not begin by selecting an asset. They should feel like they are entering their hangar.

---

## 9. Aircraft Identity Model

Each aircraft has a primary identity. The hierarchy:

- **Primary:** Registration / Tail Number
- **Secondary:** Make and Model
- **Optional:** Nickname

Example: N123AZ — Piper PA-38 Tomahawk — "Tommy"

**Design Principle:** The tail number represents the aircraft. The nickname represents the owner's relationship with it.

---

## 10. Home Screen Philosophy

The home screen should feel like a portrait of the aircraft, not a dashboard. The hierarchy:

1. Aircraft identity
2. Aircraft image
3. Ownership status
4. Recent activity
5. Actions

**Recommended Home Screen Structure**

```
[Hero Aircraft Photo]
N123AZ
Piper PA-38 Tomahawk
"Tommy"

Ownership Snapshot:
342.6 flight hours
87 flights
14 airports visited

Recent Hangar Activity:
- Flight added
- Maintenance updated
- Memory added
```

---

## 11. Hero Image Direction

**Primary Principle:** The aircraft is the hero. The hero image should represent ownership, not simply the aircraft model.

**Preferred Photography Style — use:**

- Owner-provided photos
- Full aircraft view
- Real environments
- Airport context
- Hangars
- Ramps
- Grass strips
- Sunrise / sunset lighting

**Avoid:**

- Manufacturer-style product shots
- White background aircraft renders
- Cockpit-only imagery
- Generic aviation stock photography

---

## 12. Visual Design Direction

**Overall Style:** Apple product elegance with aviation craftsmanship underneath.

The interface should feel: minimal, calm, premium, timeless, intuitive.

**Design Principle:** The interface should disappear. The aircraft, story, and ownership experience should be the focus.

---

## 13. Visual Inspiration

The design should borrow from:

- Apple product design
- Premium automotive experiences
- High-end photography apps
- Luxury ownership experiences

It should avoid:

- Cockpit dashboards
- Instrument panels
- Aviation-themed UI patterns
- Technical complexity

---

## 14. Color Direction

> Updated in v1.1 — replaces the Warm Ivory / Aviation Brass palette with a blue and white direction. The interface should read as generous white negative space, anchored by aviation blue at key identity moments.

**Primary Palette**

**Aviation Blue** — `#042C53`
- Primary brand anchor
- Sign-in and hero moments
- Depth, confidence, trust

**Sky Blue** — `#185FA5`
- Interactive accents
- Active states, links, icons
- Tags and highlights

**Cloud White** — `#FFFFFF`
- Primary background
- Clean canvas for content screens
- Negative space that lets the aircraft and story breathe

**Graphite** — `#2C2C2A`
- Text
- Contrast
- Premium feel

**Design Principle:** White space leads. Blue anchors.

Content screens (feed, story, care, fly) should be predominantly white — the aircraft photo and story are the color. Aviation Blue is reserved for identity moments: sign-in, onboarding, navigation accents, and calls to action, so it reads as premium rather than heavy.

Avoid tinting large content backgrounds in Aviation Blue; use it for anchors, not fills.

---

## 15. Typography Direction

**Primary Typography:** Modern, clean sans-serif.

Examples: SF Pro, Inter, Helvetica Neue

Characteristics: minimal, readable, premium

**Avoid:** military fonts, stencil fonts, aviation-themed display fonts. Digital Hangar should feel modern, not nostalgic.

---

## 16. Brand Personality

Digital Hangar should feel like: a knowledgeable aviation friend who understands why your airplane matters.

The personality is: calm, experienced, trustworthy, passionate, approachable.

---

## 17. Voice and Messaging

**Tone:** Professional aviation + personal connection.

**Examples**

| Avoid | Prefer |
|---|---|
| "Annual inspection due." | "Annual inspection due in 84 days. Keep your aircraft ready for the flights ahead." |
| "No flights recorded." | "Your first flight together is waiting." |
| "Upload documents." | "Keep your aircraft records organized and always within reach." |

---

## 18. Navigation Philosophy

The aircraft is the organizing principle. Primary areas:

**Story** — the emotional layer. Includes: photos, memories, milestones, ownership history.

**Care** — the confidence layer. Includes: maintenance, documents, inspections, squawks.

**Fly** — the adventure layer. Includes: flights, routes, destinations, flight history.

---

## 19. AI Philosophy

AI should be an intelligent layer across the product. AI is not the brand.

- Avoid: "AI-powered aircraft management platform"
- Prefer: "Your aircraft assistant."

AI examples:

- Story: "Tell me the history of this aircraft."
- Care: "When is my next inspection due?"
- Fly: "Show me my favorite destinations."

---

## 20. Website Design Direction

**Primary Goal:** Convert existing aircraft owners into Digital Hangar users.

**Primary Message:** Every airplane has a story. Give yours a home.

**Primary CTA:** Create Your Hangar

**Hero Direction:** Aircraft-first. The aircraft should be the visual centerpiece. Avoid making the website about pilots or lifestyle imagery. The aircraft is the emotional anchor.

**Website Structure**

- **Hero** — Aircraft image. Headline: "Your airplane. Your story. Your digital hangar." CTA: Create Your Hangar.
- **Section: Built for Aircraft Owners** — "For the pilot who knows every detail. For the owner who remembers every flight. For the airplane that deserves a history."
- **Section: Three Pillars** — Story: preserve the moments. Care: know your airplane. Fly: remember every adventure.

---

## 21. Community Philosophy

Community is a future extension. Digital Hangar v1 is a private ownership experience.

The first relationship is: Owner ↔ Aircraft

Future community should be organized around aircraft stories, not generic social networking. Examples: aircraft type communities, owner groups, shared experiences, maintenance knowledge.

---

## 22. Sign-In Experience

> New in v1.1.

**Primary Principle**

The sign-in screen is the first handshake between the owner and their hangar. It should feel like an invitation into a home, not a login gate.

**Design Direction**

- Full-bleed hero moment: aircraft or hangar imagery filling the screen, with Aviation Blue as the anchor tone rather than a plain white form.
- Brand mark and tagline ("Your aircraft. Your story. Your home.") presented over the image — not just a logo centered on white.
- Sign-in options read as an invitation, not a form: "Continue with Apple" and "Continue with Google" as solid white pill buttons floating over the hero image.
- A warm line for new owners — "New here? Create your hangar." — given equal visual weight to the sign-in buttons, not buried below legal text.

**Avoid**

- Plain white background with a centered logo and two gray outline buttons.
- Leading with Terms of Service / Privacy Policy as the most visible secondary content.
- Generic OAuth-button styling with no aviation warmth or brand presence.

---

## 23. Brand Principles Summary

Digital Hangar should always feel:

- **Aircraft First** — The airplane is the hero.
- **Personal** — Every aircraft has a unique story.
- **Premium** — The experience should feel beautifully crafted.
- **Accessible** — Every GA owner belongs.
- **Calm** — The UI should disappear.
- **Trustworthy** — Aviation requires confidence.

---

## Final Brand Statement

Digital Hangar is the digital home for general aviation aircraft owners — a beautiful, intelligent place to preserve an aircraft's story, care for it with confidence, and celebrate every flight.
