# Digital Hangar
## Product Requirements Document (PRD)

**Version:** 0.1
**Status:** MVP Definition
**Product Name:** Digital Hangar
**User Experience:** My Digital Hangar
**Platform:** iOS Mobile Application
**Initial Community:** Piper PA-38 Tomahawk Owners

---

## 1. Executive Summary

Digital Hangar is a mobile application designed to give aircraft owners a digital home for their airplane.

Every airplane has a story: the day it was purchased, the first flight with a new owner, the maintenance challenges overcome, the upgrades installed, the milestones achieved, the memories created.

Digital Hangar helps owners preserve that story while creating a foundation for aircraft ownership knowledge sharing.

The aircraft itself is the central object. The owner's experiences, maintenance history, and community knowledge accumulate around that aircraft.

Digital Hangar is not intended to replace aircraft logbooks, maintenance systems, or aviation forums. Instead, it creates a new ownership experience focused on aircraft history, connection, and shared knowledge.

---

## 2. Product Vision

**Vision Statement:** Every airplane deserves a digital home.

Digital Hangar enables aircraft owners to preserve their aircraft's story and connect with other owners who share their passion.

---

## 3. Product Principles

### 3.1 Aircraft First

The aircraft is the primary object in Digital Hangar. The product is organized around aircraft identity and history.

The user is not creating posts about an airplane. The user is contributing to the airplane's story.

Example — instead of: "Azhar posted a maintenance update." Digital Hangar thinks: "N123AZ has a new chapter in its history."

### 3.2 Story Over Paperwork

Aircraft ownership is emotional. The product should capture meaningful ownership moments rather than requiring users to perform administrative data entry.

Examples: first flight, first passenger, first cross-country, repairs, modifications, upgrades, lessons learned.

Structured data should naturally emerge from these stories.

### 3.3 Community Through Ownership

Digital Hangar is not intended to be another social media platform. Community exists because owners share something meaningful: the same aircraft type, similar ownership challenges, aviation passion.

Aircraft ownership creates the connection.

### 3.4 AI as an Assistant

AI should help owners understand their aircraft history and collective knowledge.

AI should: summarize, organize, retrieve information, identify patterns.

AI should not: replace mechanics, diagnose aircraft issues, provide authoritative maintenance guidance.

### 3.5 Privacy by Design

Aircraft ownership information belongs to the owner. Digital Hangar should provide clear privacy controls, transparent data usage, owner-controlled sharing.

The product should avoid advertising-driven data practices.

---

## 4. Target Users

### 4.1 New Aircraft Owners

**Description:** Individuals who recently purchased an aircraft and are learning ownership responsibilities.

**Needs:** a place to document their aircraft, help remembering ownership history, access to experienced owners, confidence in managing ownership.

### 4.2 Experienced Aircraft Owners

**Description:** Owners with years of aircraft knowledge and history.

**Needs:** preserve aircraft stories, share knowledge, document accumulated experience, leave a legacy for future owners.

---

## 5. Product Positioning

**Brand:** Digital Hangar
**Experience:** My Digital Hangar

The user experience should feel personal: "Welcome back to your hangar."

---

## 6. Core Value Proposition

Digital Hangar provides aircraft owners a digital home for their airplane — a place where aircraft history is preserved, memories are captured, maintenance experiences are recorded, and knowledge is shared.

---

## 7. MVP Goals

The MVP should validate:

1. Aircraft owners want a dedicated digital home for their aircraft.
2. Owners enjoy documenting aircraft stories.
3. Ownership organization provides meaningful value.
4. Aircraft-type communities can naturally form around shared ownership.

---

## 8. MVP Non-Goals

The MVP will not attempt to build: aircraft logbook replacement, pilot logbook, maintenance tracking system replacement, flight planning application, aircraft marketplace, full discussion forum, fleet management platform.

---

## 9. Primary User Experience

**First Launch:** the user should immediately understand: "This is where my airplane lives."

**Primary actions:**
- **Add My Aircraft** — create an aircraft profile.
- **Find an Aircraft** — discover existing aircraft profiles.

---

## 10. Aircraft Profile

The aircraft profile is the foundation of Digital Hangar.

**Required Fields:** registration number, manufacturer, model, primary photo.

**Optional Fields:** nickname, year, serial number, engine information, home airport, purchase date, ownership story.

**Aircraft Identity** — primary identity example: N123AZ, 1979 Piper PA-38-112 Tomahawk. Optional personality layer: "Bluebird." Aircraft nicknames are supported but not required.

---

## 11. Aircraft Story

The aircraft timeline is the emotional center of Digital Hangar.

**Memories** — examples: purchase story, first flight, first passenger, cross-country flights, personal milestones. Data: title, description, date, photos.

**Maintenance Stories** — examples: annual inspections, repairs, modifications, upgrades. Data: description, date, photos, notes.

**Milestones** — examples: 100 hours owned, first year of ownership, major upgrades, restoration work.

---

## 12. Aircraft Care

Ownership support features provide practical value.

**Squawks** — owners can record aircraft issues. Example: Issue — intermittent radio static; Status — Open; Resolution — antenna connection repaired.

**Reminders** — supported reminders: annual inspection, oil changes, insurance renewal, registration renewal.

---

## 13. Aircraft Communities

**MVP Community Model:** aircraft ownership automatically determines community membership. Example: a Piper PA-38 owner automatically belongs to "Piper PA-38 Owners." No additional setup required.

**MVP Community Features:** browse similar aircraft, view shared aircraft stories, discover other owners.

**Future Community Features:** comments, questions and answers, discussions, regional owner groups, owner collaboration.

---

## 14. Privacy Model

Aircraft visibility options:

- **Private** — visible only to the owner.
- **Community** — visible to owners of the same aircraft type.
- **Public** — discoverable by anyone.

---

## 15. AI Strategy

AI is a supporting capability. The value comes from aircraft history and community knowledge.

**MVP AI Capability — "Ask About My Aircraft":** "When was my last oil change?" / "What issues have I tracked?" / "Summarize my aircraft history."

**Future AI Capabilities** — Community Intelligence: "What issues have PA-38 owners reported?" Ownership Assistant: "Things other owners discuss during annual inspections."

---

## 16. Platform Requirements

**MVP Platform:** iOS mobile application.

**Future Platforms:** Android, web companion, public aircraft pages.

---

## 17. Expansion Vision

**Multiple Aircraft** — a user's personal hangar. Example: My Digital Hangar — Piper PA-38, Cessna 150, RV-7.

**Additional Aircraft Communities** — potential expansion: Cessna, Piper, Mooney, Cirrus, Experimental aircraft.

**Aircraft Lineage** — long-term vision: aircraft history follows the aircraft. Example: "Bluebird has been owned by four Digital Hangar members over 40 years."

---

## 18. Success Criteria

The MVP succeeds when: owners create aircraft profiles, owners add meaningful stories, owners return to update their aircraft, owners find value in shared knowledge.

**Primary measurement:** amount of aircraft history and knowledge created. Not: downloads, likes, followers.

---

## 19. Product North Star

Help every aircraft owner preserve the story of their airplane and learn from the owners who came before them.
